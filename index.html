    ACHIEVEMENTS

    HOW TO PLAY

    MOVE

    WASD or Arrows. On touch, drag the left side of the screen.

    HIT

    J, Z or Left click. When the boss is inside the ring around you, the ring lights up and your hit lands. Each hit takes its own press.

    DODGE

    Space, K, X or Right click. A quick dash that passes through anything, in the direction you're moving.

    ON BEAT

    A hit that lands right on the beat does double damage. Watch the lamp in the corner, or just listen to the kick drum.

    SURGE

    Dodge through an attack to store a charge, up to three. Your next hit spends them all for big damage. Getting hit loses them.

    READ THE ARENA

    Hollow means warning. Solid means pain. Dashed outlines show where an attack will land. You are the white shape, which you can change in the Character menu.

    WEAPONS

    Beat wave 1 to unlock switching. Choose one in the Weapons menu, or press Q in a fight to swap between Pulse, Quick Pulse, Orb Shooter and Heavy Slam.

    COLOR SWITCH

    Press C at any time in a fight to jump to the next neon color, or pick any color you like in Settings.
